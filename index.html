About

Sofia Laurent is an international fashion model based between Paris and New York. Known for her striking features and versatile presence, she has graced the pages of leading publications and walked the runways for some of the world's most celebrated designers.

With a background in dance and a degree in art history, Sofia brings a unique artistic sensibility to every project. She speaks French, English, and Italian fluently.
